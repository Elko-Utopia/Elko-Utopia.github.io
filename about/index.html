2026-06-13

🪐About Me

Hello, and welcome to this website. I'm Elko, and I am currently a student majoring in Animation and Concept Design at SCAD.

If you would like to view my résumé, please visit my LinkedIn.

I am a left-handed concept design student with color vision deficiency. I am passionate about drawing, worldbuilding, and technology. Here, I share my artwork and blog posts.

I hope to treat everyone with sincerity and enthusiasm. My pursuit of art is centered on originality, independence, and meaningful ideas. The Star of Embarkation is the long-term hard science fiction project I have been building. I have devoted seven years to its development, and the story has gone through multiple complete revisions and reconstructions. I may share more of my plans and progress on the blog in the future.

The story of The Star of Embarkation begins when the last survivors of humanity escape a war-torn Earth aboard a spacecraft and arrive on an alien world inhabited by another intelligent species. There, they discover that the planet's lifeforms can suppress electricity and thermal energy, causing the entire logic of human industrial civilization to become nearly ineffective. When faced with this alien species, the leaders of the survivors offer five fundamentally different answers: coexistence, peace, conquest, integration, and exploitation. As their ideologies diverge, cooperation gradually gives way to conflict, and they ultimately become enemies.

Will humanity learn to coexist with another intelligent species, or will it repeat the fate of Earth on the Star of Embarkation?

🖥️ About This Website

This website began as a simple experiment for learning, documenting, and creating.

I built this website using Astro because tools such as Adobe Portfolio do not allow users to save their websites locally. This inability to export projects, effectively forcing users to continue paying subscriptions, is clearly an anti-user design created to protect corporate interests. As a result, I prefer free and open-source tools that provide the features users actually need. These tools give me greater confidence and security in preserving my creative work.

I use AI tools for programming, which is also one of the ways I learn to code. I will continue adding more features to improve the browsing experience.

This website is still under active development. Thank you for visiting!

🪐关于我

你好，欢迎来到这个网站。我是托邦，我目前是SCAD动画与概念设计专业的学生。

如果您想查看我的简历，请访问我的领英

我目前是一个左撇子并且色弱的概念设计学生，我热爱绘画、创作世界观与电子技术。在这里，我将分享我的作品和博客。

我希望对每一个人真诚、热忱，我对艺术的追求是独特、独立、有宏大内涵，《启航星》正是我在构建的长期硬科幻项目，我目前已经持续在上面投入 7 年的创作，这个故事经历了多次的彻底颠覆和重构。我后续可能会在博客更新我的计划。

《启航星》的故事从人类最后的幸存者乘飞船逃离战火中的地球，抵达拥有另一种智慧生物的异星开始——他们发现这颗星球的生物能够压制电力与热能，人类工业文明的整套逻辑在这里近乎失效。 面对异星的种族，幸存者的领导者给出了五种截然不同的答案：共生、和平、征服、融合、利用。这些领导者因为各自的思想从合作，分歧最终反目成仇。

人类终究会学会与异族共生，还是在启航星重演地球的结局？

🖥️关于这个网站

这个网站起初只是一次简单的尝试，用来学习、记录与创作。

我使用Astro建立这个网站，是因为Adobe Portfolio 等工具无法把我的网页保存到本地，这种无法导出迫使用户一直续费的设计，明显是企业为了自我保护而设计的反用户功能。所以我更加倾向于自由开源，提供真正用户需要的功能的开源工具，这些工具让我对我的创作更加有安全感。

我通过使用 AI 工具来编程，这也是我学习编程的一个方式。我会尝试给网站添加更多功能，使浏览体验更好。

网站仍在持续完善，感谢你的到访!
