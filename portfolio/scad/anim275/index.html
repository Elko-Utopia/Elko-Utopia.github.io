    ANIM 275: Core Principles of Animated Storytelling and Concept Development ANIM 275：动画叙事与概念开发

    2026-11-19
    SCADConcept Art概念设计

    Instructor: Scott Wright

    I. Pre-Quarter Assignment: Cemetery Composition

    This piece shows a boy trying to bring flowers to a friend who passed away, while cemetery security keeps him from getting near the grave. He climbs into a tree, holding onto the flowers and reaching toward the headstone.

    My main goal here was practicing light, using value and warm/cool contrast to pull the eye toward the boy and the key parts of the scene. It was also the first time I tried painting primarily with planes of color. I used to rely heavily on lines to build forms, so switching to broad shapes and value relationships felt unfamiliar and took some effort to wrestle with.

    II. The Lost Spaceship

    With a spaceship stranded in the desert as the prompt, I wanted to push both the scale gap between the ship and the lone figure and the sense that the ship belongs to a completely different era from its surroundings.

    I sketched nine thumbnails to test different compositions. Some made the ship tower over a tiny figure, while others leaned into steeper perspective to emphasize volume and space. I picked the seventh thumbnail at the bottom left for the final painting: a rough desert canyon with a transparent crystalline ship resting in the middle. Smooth crystal against jagged rock gives a clear material contrast, and the ship's own glow cuts against the shadowed canyon.

    Through this project, the 7:2:1 principle started to click for me as a practical way to control focal hierarchy through visual proportions. Painting rock and crystal also pushed me further away from line drawing and into shaping volume with broad light and shadow masses.

    III. The Mimetic Braincase

    Completed: October 8, 2026

    For the prop assignment, I brought in an enemy from my original series, The Star of Embarkation. It looks like a regular suitcase on the outside, but inside sits a transplanted human brain that remotely operates multiple clones through neural interfaces. Here I focused on mechanical construction, perspective, and rendering contrasting materials.

    The piece combines mechanical arms, a leather shell, and twin gun barrels underneath, so I spent time separating the metal and leather finishes while keeping the joints believable. Because the sketch had too many interlocking parts to eyeball in perspective, I built a simplified Maya blockout to nail down the structure and cast shadows, then painted the final 2D rendering over that reference.

    The hive concept was inspired by Ender's Game: as long as the hidden braincase stays intact, it can keep running its clones. Neural links can be implanted in both the brain and spinal cord so a clone can keep moving even after a fatal head wound, and any intel it gathers still feeds back to the host. Hidden spider-like legs let the case slip away and hide almost anywhere, though all clones must stay within its circular signal radius since a disconnected clone cannot reconnect on its own.

    IV. Ori Style Deconstruction and Reinterpretation

    This assignment asked us to study the visual style of an existing game or film and design an original character that feels native to that world. I picked the Ori series because I have always loved its concept art.

    I started by breaking down the shapes, silhouettes, and color palettes of Ori's characters into basic geometry, while looking at how the 7:2:1 principle guides their color ratios. From there, I designed a friendly jeweler who could travel alongside the protagonist. While sketching and painting the front view, I worked on matching the proportions and colors of the series and spent extra time trying to get a soft, furry texture right. Finally, I placed the character into an Ori-inspired environment and tested depth of field to separate foreground, midground, and background.

    V. Storyboard from The Star of Embarkation

    Completed: October 5, 2026

    For this storyboard, I adapted a turning point from The Star of Embarkation where Dyson and Lucian split over irreconcilable beliefs. I drafted nine panels at first, then trimmed the beat down to six so the tension stayed tight.

    I leaned on props and physical gestures to carry their relationship. The Embarkation Plan emblem stands for the ideals they once shared, while Lucian pointing his gun at the map makes his intent to wipe out the elves through war unmistakable. As the argument boils over, Dyson walks out and drops the emblem behind him to make the break visually immediate.

    That ending was adjusted specifically for this assignment. In the main storyline of The Star of Embarkation, Dyson never discards the emblem. Both Dyson and Lucian believe they are the true voice of the Embarkation Plan, and neither will ever admit to abandoning its original ideals. Packing a layered argument into six frames through framing, gesture, and a single recurring prop was a tough but rewarding exercise.

    VI. Cinematic Deconstruction and Translation

    Completed: November 19, 2026

    For the final assignment, I broke down film framing across composition, perspective, lighting, and color, then carried those lessons into three original concept scenes.

    The first image focuses on using atmospheric perspective to pull open spatial depth and convey the towering height of the gatehouse. The second image uses multiple forms of contrast to separate the foreground from the background. The third image is an overhead version of the second scene, re-staging the same forest encounter from a high vantage point.

    一、课前作业：墓园构图

    画里是一个想给去世朋友献花的男孩，但墓园安保拦着不让他靠近。他爬到树上，手里攥着花，试着往朋友墓碑的方向探过去。

    这张图我主要在练光线，靠明暗和冷暖对比把视线引到人物和关键区域上。这也是我第一次尝试直接用色块和面来画。以前我画画习惯先用线条搭结构，这次换成了先铺大色块、再整理明暗关系，刚开始很不顺手，画起来挺费劲的。

    二、迷失的飞船

    这次的题目是沙漠里迷失的飞船，我想把飞船和人的体型差距，还有飞船跟环境之间的时代反差画出来。

    前期我试了九张构图草图，有的把飞船压得非常巨大、人缩得很小，有的则拉强透视去强调体积和空间。最后挑了左下角的第七张继续画。画面放在一座粗糙的岩石峡谷里，正中间停着一艘透明晶体构成的飞船。光滑的晶体和粗糙的岩石能拉开材质反差，飞船自身的光也能跟阴暗的峡谷形成对比，让人一眼觉得它不属于这个地方。

    做完这个项目后，我对构图里的 7:2:1 原则有了更实在的理解，知道怎么靠不同元素占画面的比例来控制视觉重心。画岩石和晶体也逼着我放下线条习惯，改用大面积的明暗色块去交代体积和质感。

    三、虫后生命之箱

    完成日期：2026年10月8日

    道具作业里，我把原创系列《启航星》里的机械敌人“虫后生命之箱”拿来做细化。它表面像只普通手提箱，里面其实装着人的大脑，再靠神经接口远程操控多个克隆体。这次作业我主要练机械结构、透视和不同材质的画法。

    箱体由机械臂、皮革外壳和底部的双枪管组成，我重点区分了金属和皮革的质感，也反复调了零件之间的连接。草图阶段的透视和零件关系太复杂，直接手画很容易歪，所以我先在 Maya 里搭了简化模型来定结构和投影，再导进软件里照着参考完成最终的二维上色。

    控制方式的灵感来自《安德的游戏》里的虫群结构，只要隐藏在暗处的本体没被打掉，就能一直操控克隆体行动。神经接口可以同时埋在大脑和脊髓里，哪怕克隆体头部受损也能继续活动，而且死后还能把情报回传给本体。箱子底下藏着蜘蛛一样的机械足，逃跑和藏匿能力都很强，但所有克隆体必须在以箱子为圆心的有效范围内保持连接，一旦断线就无法重连，这也是它的弱点。

    四、奥日风格拆解与再创作

    这个项目要求挑一部自己喜欢的动画或游戏，拆解它的美术风格，再设计一个能融进去的原创角色。我一直很喜欢《奥日》系列的概念美术，所以选了它。

    我先从角色的形体、轮廓和配色入手，用几何图形去拆解原设里的造型规律，也观察了画面里 7:2:1 原则在色彩和元素比例上的用法。之后我设计了自己的角色，是一位性格友善、可以和主角同行的珠宝商。画草图和正面设定的时候，我一直在调形体比例和配色，也花了不少功夫去表现身体柔软毛绒的质感。最后把角色放进一个同风格的场景里，顺便试着用景深拉开前景、中景和背景的层次。

    五、启航星故事分镜

    完成日期：2026年10月5日

    这次分镜我画的是《启航星》里的一段冲突，讲迪崇和炽洛因为理念彻底对立而决裂的过程。原本画了九格草稿，后来为了把节奏收紧，删减成了六格。

    画面里主要靠道具和动作来交代关系。启航计划的徽章象征两人曾经共同的理想与信任，炽洛拿枪指着地图则直接点出他打算靠战争消灭精灵的想法。随着矛盾升级，迪崇转身离开，在这次作业里我画了他把徽章丢在原地，用来直接表现两人决裂。

    这个结尾只是为了作业精简做的改编。 在《启航星》的正式故事里，迪崇并不会扔掉徽章。他和炽洛都觉得自己才代表真正的启航计划，哪怕走到彻底对立的地步，谁也不会承认自己背离了最初的理想。把这么一段复杂的对手戏压缩进六格里，全靠机位、动作和道具来把情绪串起来，过程挺有挑战性。

    六、电影镜头拆解与转译

    完成日期：2026年11月19日

    结课作业是先拆解成熟电影里的镜头语言，包括构图、透视、光线和色彩，再把学到的东西用到三张自己的概念图里。

    第一张图着重用空气透视拉开空间纵深，同时借着空气透视感去衬出城楼的高度。第二张图则通过多种形式的对比，把前景和背景的差别强调出来。第三张图是俯视版的第二张图，换到高角度重新交代同一组林间场景和人物关系。
