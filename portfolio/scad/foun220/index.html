    Project 1: Master Drawing Remix

    The purpose of this project is for students to imitate a master's work to learn their techniques. Students need to incorporate a master's work into their drawing, add their own original elements, and keep the composition as harmonious as possible.

    "A Faith Beyond Understanding"

    22 × 30 in Gray Rives BFK Paper, Black and White Charcoal

    This artwork participated in the SCAD Foundation Studies Honors Exhibition 2026 and was selected as a finalist in the awards. This drawing incorporates Albrecht Dürer's "Praying Hands" and features a goat head drawn in an imitation of his style. I drafted it on gray paper using black and white charcoal. Meanwhile, the clothing patterns of the figure are inspired by traditional Tibetan attire; I attempted to use its rich decorative details and intricate patterns to add an exotic atmosphere to the composition.

    During the conceptualization process, I strove to create an atmosphere where tranquility and unfamiliarity coexist, which is why I chose the elements in the composition: prayer, a goat, and ethnic minority clothing. The praying hands possess a sense of the sacred, while the goat is a symbol of the devil in Western culture, and the Tibetan clothing also carries an exotic vibe and a sense of the unfamiliar.

    Secondly, in terms of composition, the goat head, the hands, and the clothing serve as focal points, forming a stable triangular structure. The goat's leftward gaze also guides the flow of the image, preventing the top-left corner from feeling empty. Drawing on gray paper better mimics Dürer's original work and effectively enriches the image.

    Project 3: Dramatic Mood Narrative

    In this project, students are required to take a photograph with distinct light and shadow and a dramatic perspective angle, and then create a derivative work based on it, focusing on practicing perspective and the sense of lighting.

    "Melancholy"

    22 × 30 in Blue Rives BFK Paper, Black and White Charcoal

    This drawing primarily conveys an oppressive atmosphere. It uses a blue tone as the base and mainly relies on white for highlights. A major difficulty with this drawing is that the paper has high saturation and is inherently a mid-tone, while mixing white and black charcoal can make the image look muddy, leaving very little room for error when making corrections. My composition referenced a cinematic shot from "Requiem for a Dream." RequiemforaDream.webp The upper half of this cinematic shot is enveloped in a black gradient, presenting an oppressive atmosphere. This method not only aligns with the theme of my drawing but also intensifies the "infinite" feel of the buildings in the frame.

    I first edited my photo, then drew based on the edited photo.

    At the same time, there was an additional output during my shooting process: a night view of a church in Savannah. This photo also has a great atmosphere, but because it was too complex and charcoal struggles to render subtle details, I ultimately abandoned it. However, I still consider it a meaningful piece of photography.

    Project 4: Imaginary Places

    The theme of this project is "Surrealism." In this project, students need to select elements to express a specific word and draw it in color. For this theme, the keyword I chose was "Sense of Smell."

    "Flavor"

    22 × 20 in Brown Rives BFK Paper, 24-Color Dry Pastels

    This drawing is my first attempt at pastel painting. To me, lavender and lemons seem to be completely unrelated items, but they hold unique properties in my mind—neither lavender nor lemons are suitable for direct consumption, yet they both possess very distinct, unique scents and colors. Furthermore, lemon yellow and lavender purple are complementary colors, and this intense collision of colors will inevitably create a striking contrast.

    In this artwork, I attempted to draw juicy lemons and lush, overlapping layers of lavender. Right in the center of the composition, I drew a woman with curly orange-yellow hair.

    The human figure is the element that can most evoke emotion; at the same time, I am also using the curls of the hair to convey a sense of flow. The overlapping relationships among the lavender, the lemons, and the hair, along with the foreground/background detail contrast of the lavender, also make the image more three-dimensional and diverse.

    In post-production, I added a vignette to the edges of the image, making the exact center the focal point of the composition, which also enhances the sense of spatial depth.

    Project 1: 混合大师之作

    这个项目的目的是让学生模仿大师作品以学习其技法。学生需要在画面中使用一个大师作品，并原创自己的元素，并且让画面尽可能保持和谐。

    《理解之外的信仰》

    55.88×76.2cm灰色RivesBFK画纸 黑白双色炭条

    这幅作品参加了SCAD的展览Foundation Studies Honors Exhibition 2026，并在评奖活动入围决赛。 这幅画结合了丢勒的《祈祷的手》，并模仿其风格绘制了羊头。我在灰色的纸张上起稿，使用了黑白炭笔。同时人物的服饰的纹样灵感来源于藏族传统服饰，我试图让其丰富的装饰细节和繁复的纹饰为画面增添异域感。

    在创作的构思过程中，我力求营造一种宁静与陌生并存的氛围，也是因此我选择了画面中的元素：祈祷，山羊，少数民族服装。祈祷的手有一种神圣感，而山羊在西方文化中是恶魔的象征，藏族的服饰也有异域氛围和陌生感。

    其次，在画面构图中，羊头，手，服饰分别作为画面焦点，构成了三角形的稳定构图。羊头向左的视线也引导画面走向，让画面左上角不显得空旷。而在灰色纸张上作画更好的模仿了丢勒的原作，也有效丰富了画面。

    Project 3: 戏剧化场景

    在这个项目，学生需要拍摄一个光影明显，透视角度大的照片，并对其进行二次创作，着重练习透视和光感。

    《惆怅》

    55.88×76.2cm蓝色RivesBFK画纸 黑白双色炭条

    这张画主要表达压抑氛围。使用了蓝色调为基底，主要使用白色提亮。 这幅画非常大的难点就是纸张饱和度高而且本身就是中间色，而白色和黑色炭笔混合会让画面变灰，所以修改的容错很少。我的画面构图参考了《梦之安魂曲》的电影镜头 RequiemforaDream.webp 这个电影镜头的上半部分被黑色渐变笼罩，呈现出一股压抑氛围。这种方法不但契合我的绘画主题，而且可以让画面楼房的“无限”感更强烈。

    我先编辑了我的照片，再根据照片创作。

    同时，在我拍摄过程中也有额外产出：萨凡纳的教堂夜景。这个照片也很有氛围感，但是因为过于复杂，碳条很难表现细微细节，最终放弃。但是我仍然认为这是一个有意义的摄影作品。

    Project 4: 超现实主义

    这个项目的主题是“超现实”。在这个项目，学生需要选取元素去表达一个词，并且需要用彩色绘制。对这个主题，我选取的关键词是“嗅觉”。

    《馥郁》

    55×50cm棕色RivesBFK画纸 彩色干性24色粉彩棒

    这幅画是我尝试的第一个粉彩画。对我而言，薰衣草和柠檬似乎是毫不相干的物品，但是它们在我心目中有独特的性质-薰衣草和柠檬都不适合直接食用，但是它们都有非常显著的独特气味和颜色。并且，柠檬黄和薰衣草紫是互补色，这种强烈碰撞的颜色必然会创造出强烈的对比。

    这个作品中，我尝试绘制多汁的柠檬和繁茂、层层叠叠的薰衣草。在画面正中心，我绘制了一个有橙黄色卷发的女人。

    人物是最能引起情绪的元素，同时，我也在利用头发的卷曲表达流动感。而薰衣草柠檬和头发的叠压关系，和薰衣草的先后细节对比也让画面更立体，更多样化。

    在后期制作，我给画面的边缘加上了晕影，这让画面的正中心成为画面焦点，也可以增强空间感。
