DIGI 130: Digital Communication DIGI 130：数字传播

    DIGI 130: Digital Communication DIGI 130：数字传播

    2026-06-20
    SCADDigital Communication数字传播Branding品牌设计

    Instructor: Odilon Frazao

    DIGI 130 introduces foundational digital communication for creative professions. The course centers on research, ideation, production, and the presentation of professional identity through digital tools. In this class, I focused on building a personal brand system and completed an Adobe Photoshop certification exam as part of the digital production component.

    指导教授：Odilon Frazao

    DIGI 130 是面向创意专业的基础数字传播课程。课程内容围绕调研、构思、数字制作，以及如何通过数字工具呈现职业身份展开。在这门课中，我主要完成了个人品牌系统的建设，并将 Adobe Photoshop 认证考试作为数字制作学习的一部分。
