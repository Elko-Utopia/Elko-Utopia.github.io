    Boss Rush Boss Rush

    2026-10-18
    Personal个人创作Game Development游戏开发

    Boss Rush is the first bullet-hell game I developed independently. I used JavaScript and Microsoft MakeCode Arcade to build its mechanics, pixel art, animation effects, and supporting systems. The first version took about a week to complete, and I continued adding bosses, attack combinations, interface features, and performance improvements afterward.

    Successive boss battles form the core of the game. Each boss has a signature attack, while other attacks combine randomized main patterns and sub-patterns. The shield can block bullets or be thrown to damage enemies. Custom Boss mode lets players combine bosses and adjust their encounter settings.

    You can play v1.0.17 below and explore the combat design, animations, original pixel art, and rendering optimizations.

    Boss Combat Design

    Randomized Patterns and Signature Attacks

    I developed a pool of main patterns and smaller sub-patterns that are randomly assigned when a boss spawns. Each boss has its own signature attack, allowing repeat encounters to vary while retaining a distinct combat identity.

    LASER's regular attack sends shifting laser-projectile patterns across the arena. Players must read their trajectories and find openings while moving.

    Boss Ultimates and Enraged Phases

    Bosses enter the enraged phase below 30% HP, upgrading their attacks. DANMAKU specializes in dense rotating projectiles, while WALL changes the speed and reverses the direction of its ultimate attack during the enraged phase. Version 1.0.17 reduced DANMAKU's ultimate density and rotation speed while strengthening WALL's and RUSH's enraged attacks.

    The summoning ultimate introduces additional enemies, turning a single-boss fight into a multi-target encounter where players must manage movement, projectiles, and summoned threats at once.

    RUSH's ultimate attack re-targets the player and changes its charge direction each time it exits the screen.

    LASER's enraged ultimate creates a dense attack built from large laser orbs. The renderer automatically fills the visible gaps between adjacent orbs, removing rough seams at their edges and making the overall effect appear continuous rather than segmented.

    HYBRID DUCK can buff other bosses. Its protective orb combines with RUSH's and WALL's attacks to create more complex multi-boss patterns.

    Shield: Defense and Offense

    The shield can block projectiles and be thrown to damage enemies, inspired by Danmaku Death.

    Pixel Art from Game Source

    I created a consistent pixel-art language for the player, bosses, and projectiles. These sprites are extracted from the game source.

    Development and Systems

    Procedural Visual Effects

    Most special effects are generated procedurally, using mathematical calculations for shapes, movement, and animation. I hand-drew key player orientations and derived others with mirroring and rotation to avoid jagged pixel-art frames and trails caused by real-time rotation.

    Bullet rendering performance

    I wrote a custom bullet renderer to avoid the per-projectile overhead of MakeCode's native Sprite pipeline. In a performance comparison, it drew 4,282 bullets at 45 FPS; the native Sprite approach drew 1,531 bullets at 17 FPS. Despite rendering more bullets, the custom approach achieved about 2.6 times the frame rate.

    I draw visible bullets onto a shared screen-sized surface, avoiding a separate Sprite rendering cost for each projectile. I also concentrate precise collision checks around the player and shield. In another stress test, the game managed over 15,000 projectiles in total, most of them off-screen, while running at approximately 25 FPS.

    Course connection: I discovered MakeCode Arcade through my introduction to game development course and created the initial version of Boss Rush independently before the group project began. The game also appears on the course page.

    Boss 战斗设计

    随机弹幕与特殊攻击

    我为 Boss 制作了多种主弹幕模式和附加子模式。每次生成 Boss 时，系统会随机选择攻击组合，使同一名 Boss 在不同战斗中出现不同的弹幕变化。每个 Boss 还有自己的专属攻击，战斗风格由专属机制和随机弹幕共同构成。

    LASER 的普通激光攻击由连续变化的激光弹幕组成，玩家需要观察弹幕轨迹，在移动中寻找躲避空间。

    Boss 大招与愤怒阶段

    当 Boss 的生命值低于 30%，就会进入愤怒阶段，攻击方式随之强化。 DANMAKU 擅长密集旋转弹幕；WALL 的终极攻击在愤怒阶段会改变速度并反向运动。v1.0.17 降低了 DANMAKU 终极攻击的密度与旋转速度，同时强化了 WALL 与 RUSH 的愤怒阶段攻击。

    召唤怪的大招会召唤更多敌人，将原本的单体 Boss 战变成多目标战斗，迫使玩家同时处理移动、弹幕和召唤物。

    RUSH 的大招会在冲出屏幕后重新锁定玩家，并改变下一次冲锋方向。

    LASER 在愤怒阶段强化终极攻击，形成由大型激光球组成的密集弹幕。程序会自动填补相邻大球之间的视觉空隙，消除大球交界处的粗糙边缘，使整体光束更加连续、完整。

    HYBRID DUCK 可以强化其他 Boss。它的保护球与 RUSH、WALL 等 Boss 的攻击叠加，使多 Boss 战斗形成更复杂的弹幕组合。

    盾牌的攻防转换

    盾牌可以防御弹幕，也能投掷出去伤害敌人。这一机制的灵感来自《Danmaku Death》，使玩家能够在防御与进攻之间主动切换。

    Boss Rush 像素美术

    这些角色、Boss 和弹幕素材直接提取自游戏源代码。

    开发过程与系统

    程序生成的美术特效

    大部分特殊效果由自定义程序根据数学公式计算形状、移动轨迹与动画，再实时绘制。我为玩家预绘重要朝向的像素帧，并使用镜像与旋转制作其他方向，以避免低分辨率图像实时旋转时的锯齿和拖尾问题。

    弹幕渲染性能

    我为密集弹幕开发了自定义渲染方式，绕开逐发 Sprite 更新与绘制的额外开销。测试中，自定义方案以 45 FPS 绘制 4,282 发弹幕；原生 Sprite 方案绘制 1,531 发时只有 17 FPS。自定义方案在显示更多弹幕的同时，帧率达到后者的约 2.6 倍。

    具体来说，我将屏幕内的弹幕集中绘制到同一张画布上，不再为每发子弹承担独立 Sprite 的渲染开销；碰撞计算也只针对接近玩家和盾牌的弹幕进行。另一次压力测试中，游戏同时管理超过 15,000 发弹幕，绝大多数位于屏幕外，仍保持约 25 FPS。
